## GEN-002: Removing a source while it is scanned brings its images and preset back

Reproduction (BATCH_FILES = 1, three PNGs in the source; the progress callback
removes the source after the first file, on a second connection, as a
concurrent request would):

    removed source; live images: 0
    source removed_at: 2026-10-26 16:35:25
    live images after scan: 2
    locations: 2
    collections: [(1, 'src', 'source_preset', 1)]

Mechanism:

- `del_source` (`api/sources.py:172-184`) depends only on `live_source`;
  nothing consults `ScanManager.is_running`. The Settings page
  (`routes/settings/+page.svelte:87-97`) offers Remove while a scan runs.
- `delete_source(keep_data=True)` purges the preset, deletes the locations,
  marks the images deleted and sets `removed_at`
  (`services/source_service.py:29-69`).
- The worker started in `trigger_scan` keeps running: `_write_batch` upserts
  images and locations for `source_id` and `sync_images` sets `deleted = 0`
  (`scan_service.py:448-468`); the final transaction updates `last_scan`, and
  `get_or_create_source_preset` inserts a new preset (`scan_service.py:257-260`).
- `list_collections` does not filter on `sources.removed_at`, so the
  resurrected preset shows in the sidebar; presets cannot be deleted, and the
  source no longer appears in Settings.
- With `keep_data=false` the source row is deleted, so the next batch's INSERT
  into `images` fails on the `sources` foreign key and the scan ends in error
  (no zombie state, but a failed scan).
